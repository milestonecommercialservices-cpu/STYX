# STYX
The intelligence based Token in Codex.

Maxwell's demon, or (daemon) in this case, defines entropy in terms of chaos. STYX preserves the inevitable entropy of token generation (AI hallucination, and compression) during AI interaction in the form of either shielded/or un-shielded, encrypted/or decrypted tokens. While it may not yield the "free reversal" of entropy, it will be a more effective means and use of data that can be reassembled later to expand intelligence data and improve model/codex behavior, manifests and scripting.
